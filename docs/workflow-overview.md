# Workflow Overview

The system automatically creates weekly Instagram captions.

Step 1

Collect the latest property news from Google News RSS.

Step 2

Send article title and summary to GPT.

Step 3

Generate

- Caption
- CTA
- Emojis
- Hashtags

Step 4

Save draft into Google Docs.

Step 5

Marketing team reviews the content.

Step 6

Publish to Instagram.
